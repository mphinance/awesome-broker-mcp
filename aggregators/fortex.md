# Fortex 7 (Fortex)

## Overview

Brokerage technology vendor. Fortex says Fortex 7 ships built-in MCP servers that extend its existing APIs, so agents such as Claude Code and Codex can reach brokerage systems without a FIX integration. Retail traders cannot connect directly: this is sold to brokerages ("Contact us to explore how Fortex 7 can be deployed within your brokerage").

## How to connect

Nothing public: no endpoint, docs, auth or install. Deployment is through Fortex.

## Trading scope

The 2026-08-05 Fortex post says agents can "connect AI models with accounts, positions, orders, and market data" and "execute trading actions through natural language commands". The 2026-07-01 post lists risk, liquidity monitoring, compliance and reporting uses and does not say whether agents can trade. Tools and platforms are not listed.

## Safety / guardrails

None described on either page; the 2026-07-01 post only says "securely".

## Caveats

- Marketing posts only. No independent evidence, nothing to probe, so `server_type` is `n/a` and confidence is low.
